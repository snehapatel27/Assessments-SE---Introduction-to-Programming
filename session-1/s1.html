For this project, I would recommend the Agile methodology. Why Agile? - The project has frequently changing requirements, and Agile allows changes to be incorporated during development. - The client wants to review working features every two weeks, which matches Agile's sprint-based approach. - Agile provides continuous feedback, helping the team improve the product after each sprint. - It enables faster delivery of usable features and better collaboration with the client. Risks of Choosing the Wrong Methodology -Waterfall 1.Difficulty Handling Changes Waterfall follows a fixed plan, so changing requirements later in the project can cause delays, increased costs, and rework. 2.Late Client Feedback In Waterfall, the client usually sees the product near the end of development. If the product does not meet expectations, major changes may be required, wasting time and resources.
